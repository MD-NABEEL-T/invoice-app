# HANDOFF: GST Invoice App for Uncle's Business
=== FINAL DECISIONS (these override anything written below) ===
USERS: the uncle's son makes the bills; the uncle (older) mostly prints and may also make bills. Android phones only, English only, mobile-first with big buttons and text. One shared login that stays signed in on each phone.
BUYER: one company, KH EXPORT INDIA PRIVATE LTD, with several branch addresses (pick one per bill); an "Add buyer" option must exist. All buyers are in Tamil Nadu: CGST + SGST only. NO IGST, no inter-state logic anywhere in the DB or UI.
MONEY: integer paise; keep paise; NO rounding to the rupee. Tax per GST-rate group rounded once; CGST = floor(tax/2), SGST = remainder.
NUMBERING: invoice no like 048/26-27 assigned automatically at save (atomic), financial year from the invoice date (April to March). The son sets the starting number once. Dates are real dates, default today, editable; warn if older than the latest bill.
BILL FIELDS: buyer, branch, date, Buyer Order No (required, free text), Buyer Order Date (free text, may hold several dates), Delivery No (optional free text, can be blank or 0000), Delivery Date (default = bill date), Despatched Through (required, free text), Destination (optional), Terms of Delivery (optional). NO vehicle number.
ITEMS: live in the DB, added as buyer orders come in. HSN is free text (warn if not 4, 6 or 8 digits). GST % per item, editable per line. Unit is free text with suggestions from past units. Rate prefills from the last bill and is editable. Nothing is ever invented: HSN and GST start blank and "needs checking" until the son confirms; the son supplies them from an Excel sheet we import.
SAVED BILLS: never edited or deleted; only cancelled; a cancelled number is never reused. "Copy as new bill" exists.
PRINT: A4 layout like the legacy bill (declaration line, "For Khansa Enterprises", "Customers Seal and Signature", "Authorised Signature" at the bottom). No bank details, no signature image. At print time ask how many copies (default 3, editable); the PDF contains one page per copy. The uncle's printer is wired to a laptop, so the flow is Share PDF from the phone via Gmail, then print on the laptop.
MONTH END (Excel): (1) statement to the buyer like the HARDCOPY sheet: invoice no/date, DC no/date, PO no/date, taxable, rate, CGST, SGST, amount; (2) GST sales register like the GST sheet; (3) HSN-wise summary (HSN, qty, taxable, rate, CGST, SGST). Cancelled bills are excluded from totals.
OLD BILLS: the app starts in November 2026; adding old bills is an optional later feature.
AGENT WORKING STYLE: do one phase at a time, test it yourself, commit with a clear message, then report in at most 10 lines, with a FAIL list if anything is off.
=== END FINAL DECISIONS ===

## 2. What I analysed (3 uploaded Excel files)
- `SINGLE_FORMAT.xlsm`: invoice template for ONE GST rate. `2BILLTAX_FORMAT.xlsm`: invoice template for TWO rates. `tax_format.xlsx`: monthly sheets (Sheet1 = statement of bills to the buyer; HARDCOPY; GST = monthly sales register; CONSOLIDATE; PURCHASE; "Dublicate1" = list of ~80 item descriptions used for dropdowns).
- The macros only convert amounts to words (Indian lakh/crore style) and make a dropdown helper. They are harmless.
- Buyer in the samples: KH Exports (same GSTIN, different units/addresses: Unit III Melvisharam, and a Chennai/Poonamallee address). A buyer can have multiple addresses.
- Volume: invoice numbers run about 001 to 047 in one month (~45-50 invoices/month). April register ≈ ₹1.35 lakh/month taxable+tax, so annual turnover is roughly ₹16-20 lakh (far below ₹5 crore).
- Invoice number format: `047/25-26` (running number / financial year). FY starts 1 April.

### Test cases to reproduce (from the samples)
- Invoice 046: hair cap net 625 @5% + nozzle 500 @18%. Taxable 1125. CGST 15.625 + 45, SGST 15.625 + 45, total tax 121.25, **grand total 1246.25**.
- Invoice 047: lines 200 + 1100 + 1575 = 2875 @18%. Tax 517.50 (CGST 258.75 + SGST 258.75). **Grand total 3392.50**.
- The old sheet's "Total (round off)" label does not actually round. Ask the uncle whether he rounds to the nearest rupee.

### Problems found in his Excel
1. In the 2-rate template, CGST/SGST, the HSN summary and total tax are **hardcoded**, so changing qty or rate leaves the tax stale while the grand total changes.
2. The single-rate template applies the FIRST line's rate to the sum of all lines, which is wrong if rates are mixed.
3. Several dates in `tax_format.xlsx` look **day/month swapped** (for example serial 45692 = 4 Feb 2025 on invoice 001/25-26, which should be about 2 April). Others are plain text like "18/4/2025".
4. Some HSN codes look wrong (for example 91051900 for a hair cap net is the clocks chapter, while hair nets belong to chapter 65; Surf Excel detergent was under a soap code 3401 where detergent is normally 3402). Treat all old HSN codes as unverified.
5. Sheet titles are inconsistent (CONSOLIDATE says FEB 2023), and the item list has typos and duplicates.
6. All monthly sheets are retyped from invoices. A single source of truth is needed.

## 3. GST facts to respect (verify before relying on them)
- From **22 Sept 2025** the 12% and 28% slabs were removed. The slabs are now **0%, 5%, 18%, 40%**. Many items changed (for example soaps, toothpaste and similar personal care moved 18% to 5%). **Old sheet rates (12%, 28%, 18% for soaps) are outdated.**
- Rate and HSN must be per item but editable per invoice line, and rates need effective dates so old invoices never change.
- As far as I know, taxpayers with turnover under ₹5 crore need only **4-digit HSN** on invoices (6-digit above). Confirm with whoever files his returns.
- GST invoice numbers should run consecutively within a financial year, so invoices should be **cancelled, never deleted or edited** after finalising. Confirm with a tax professional.
- Uncle has **no CA**. HSN codes and rates cannot be certified by AI. A human (his return filer or a local tax consultant) must confirm the active items. The app should mark each item "verified" or "needs checking".
- Do not import HSN or rates blindly from the old Excel.

## 4. Decisions made (and why)
**Stack (chosen for the app's needs):**
- Frontend: **React + Vite + Tailwind** (PWA-installable).
- DB + auth: **Supabase** (Postgres, Auth, row-level security). Public signup disabled, one login for him.
- Hosting: **Cloudflare Pages** (Netlify equivalent). **NOT Vercel Hobby**, because its free plan is non-commercial and this is a business.
- No custom backend. Invoice maths runs in the browser and Postgres protects the data.
- Printing: A4 `@media print` stylesheet plus Save as PDF (phone flow: PDF then WhatsApp). Excel export via SheetJS.
- Invoices must store a **snapshot** of buyer, item, HSN and rate at issue time.
- Catches:
  - Supabase free projects **pause after ~1 week of inactivity**. Fix: scheduled keep-alive ping (GitHub Actions cron) plus an "Export all data" button (own backups).
  - Fallbacks: Firebase (never pauses, but NoSQL and weaker reports) or Neon (Postgres that scales to zero, but needs a backend).
- Rejected: Spring Boot (poor free hosting, slow), a native app (unnecessary; printing is the goal).
- Devices: uncle usually prints from a **laptop**. The phone must work too (stacked form, Save as PDF and share).
